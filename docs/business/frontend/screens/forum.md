# Forum Screens

- **Routes:** `/forum`, `/forum/create`, `/forum/my-posts`, `/forum/posts/:id`
- **Access:** Viewing is typically Public; Creation/My Posts require `USER` role.
- **Description:** Allows users to discuss topics, ask questions, and share strategies.
- **Interactions:** CRUD operations on forum posts. Pagination and filtering.
- **States:** Loading lists, empty states, form validation for creating posts.
